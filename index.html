Publications & Research

📑 Co-Author · 2nd Author · Submitted
SMMESR — Structure-Aware Multi-Modal Embeddings for Scientific Document Retrieval

SMMESR is a lightweight dual-encoder framework that represents scientific PDFs as graphs of layout elements (text blocks, equations, figures, tables, captions) and learns unified embeddings via a GNN. It fuses three modalities — text (BERT), visual (ResNet-50), and structure (3-layer GCN) — via learned softmax attention into a 512-dim embedding, indexed with FAISS for fast retrieval. Trained on 5,000 arXiv papers spanning 10 categories (2005–2024).
